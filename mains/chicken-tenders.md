# Recipe for Air-Fried Chicken Tenders

## Ingredients

- Perdue Chicken Tenders
- Any Air Fryer
- Ranch

## Instructions

- Put the amount of chicken tenders you want in the Air Fryer
- Press "Bake" and set your Air Fryer to 400°F
- Let the tenders cook for 4 minutes
- Afterwards, press "Air Fry" and set your Air Fryer to 400°F for another 4 minutes
- Enjoy with ranch!
